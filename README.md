# 📚 NoteShelf - Collaborative Book Highlights

Every book. Every insight. One place. **NoteShelf** is a modern platform designed for readers to capture, share, and collaborate on book Notes, highlights and takeaways.

## Deployed Project Link
https://note-shelf.vercel.app/


## 🌟 Project Overview

NoteShelf is a collaborative library system that allows users to organize their reading Notes by chapters and categories. Whether it's a quote, a personal note, or a key takeaway, NoteShelf provides a structured and beautiful interface to store and discover wisdom from literature. You don't have to read the entire book you can easily read the NOtes, highlights and takeaways by your friends or other users.

## 🚀 Features

- **📖 Book Library**: Browse and manage a collection of books.
- **📑 Chapter Organization**: Notes are neatly organized by chapters for easy reference.
- **💡 Categorized Highlights**:
  - **Quotes**: Direct lines from the book that the reader found interesting.
  - **Notes**: The highlighted text by the reader.
  - **Takeaways**: Personal thoughts and reflections of the reader by reading the text.
- **👤 User Profiles**: Shows your profile and contributed highlights.
- **🔒 Secure Authentication**: Login via Google, Microsoft Email ID  and manually with your email and password.
- **👍 Upvoting System**: Interact with the community by upvoting useful highlights reader founds.
- **🌓 Dark/Light Mode**: A theme-aware UI designed for comfortable reading day or night.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Framework**: [Next.js 15+](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router & Turbopack)
- **Language**: [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Database**: [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/) (via [Prisma ORM](https://www.prisma.io/))
- **Authentication**: [NextAuth.js v5](https://authjs.dev/)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Deployment**: [Vercel](https://vercel.com/)
